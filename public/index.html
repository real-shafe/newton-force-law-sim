    About

    A browser port of simulation.py, a Python spring–mass simulator built on Newton’s second law, F = ma. As in the original, forces, integrators, collision rules and scenes are separate pieces you can swap. Units are SI; Orbits, Figure-eight, Charges and the Thermal lattice use scaled units (G = kₑ = k_B = 1). Everything runs in your browser.
